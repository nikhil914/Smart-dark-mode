What the night sky looks like from a dark-sky reserve

By Asha Rao · 9 min read

Away from city light, the sky changes completely. The Milky Way stops being a rumour and becomes a bright band of stars you can read a map by. Your eyes need about twenty minutes to adapt, and one glance at a white screen undoes it.

Red light keeps your night vision. A white screen resets it in seconds. 
exposure = 500 / focal_length  # seconds before stars trail

Most reserves ask visitors to dim every screen they carry, which is where a good dark mode earns its keep.
